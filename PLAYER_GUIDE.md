# Grow With Friends: player guide

You owe the Boss. One to four of you work it off in his factory: buy seeds at his window, grow them in the trays,
deposit the product down the chute. Every shift has a payment due. Pay it and the shift ends and the number goes up.
A run is four shifts for one worker, five for two, six for three or four. The last one is the final notice: pay it
and the debt is cleared (PAID IN FULL). Miss any payment and the run starts over.

## Host and join
- **Host:** type a name, press **Open the floor**. The first time, Windows asks for administrator permission
  ("Windows Command Processor"). Say yes: it adds one firewall rule, UDP port 7777, for this game only. Say no and
  you still host, but friends may not get in; the menu then offers **Allow UDP 7777 through Windows Firewall**.
  The first start of the game may also bring up a Windows Security window about networks: Allow access.
- **Join on the same network:** the host's floor shows under **Floors open nearby**. Double-click it.
- **Join by IP:** type the host's address under **Host IP** (the host's menu shows "Your address for friends"),
  keep port 7777, press **Report for shift**. Over the internet the host forwards UDP 7777 on their router to their
  PC and gives out their public address. Up to four workers. A friend can join mid-shift.
- **Run codes:** every run has a four-character code on the alley board. The host types one into **Run code**, or
  presses **THIS WEEK** for the code everyone gets this week, and deals the same run: the same conditions, jobs,
  order of trouble and crates. Blank means a random run.
- If the host leaves, the floor closes for everyone.

## The shift
You start in the alley. The shift starts when everyone stands in the back of the van (the host's Enter leaves
without the rest). The board on the alley wall shows the last shift, the next one and your record.

1. **Buy:** E at the supply window. Seeds and favors come out of cash on hand. You start with $150.
2. **Plant:** E on an empty tray with a seed packet in hand.
3. **Water:** pick up a can, E at the tank to fill it, E on the tray to pour. Dry soil stops growth.
4. **Wait:** it grows in stages. Keep it wet.
5. **Harvest:** E with empty hands. A bundle hung on a drying rack in the grow hall for 45 s sells for 40% more.
6. **Deposit:** E at the chute, or throw it in. Each deposit fills the payment bar and your cash on hand.

The clock is five minutes. More strains open by shift: Golden Kush at two, Night Shift and Black Damp at three,
Floor Brick at four. From shift three the board posts one condition a shift (a short clock, overtime, a slick floor),
two on the final notice, where the Boss also looks at half time: under 40% deposited and the payment goes up a
tenth. Each shift has one optional job: meet it for $60. Favors (faster growth, bigger cans, a better cut) are on the
window's second tab. Between shifts the host presses NEXT SHIFT, or START OVER after a miss.

## Keys
| Key | Does | Key | Does |
|---|---|---|---|
| WASD / arrows | move | E / left mouse | use, pick up |
| Shift | sprint | Q / G | drop |
| Space | jump | right mouse / R | throw |
| Ctrl / C | crouch | F | shove |
| V (hold) | talk | middle mouse / X | ping a spot |
| T | chat | 1-4 | gestures |
| Esc | break menu | Enter | start the shift (host) |

Keys go by position: on another keyboard layout, OPTIONS shows yours. Esc does not stop the clock.

## When it goes wrong
The first trouble comes a minute into a shift, the next one 75 to 140 seconds after the last one ends, never the
same kind twice running. Each one warns you first.

| Trouble | You see / hear | Do this | Nobody does |
|---|---|---|---|
| The Boss walks the floor | INSPECTION and a countdown, his keys | Empty hands. Keep moving. Or stay out of his sight. | Anyone near him holding a bundle (taken) or standing still 3 s is written up: $10. |
| Power cut | The lights flicker, then go | Hold E on the fuse box, on the wall behind the tank. | Dark for 40 s, nothing grows. Night Shift grows faster. |
| Leak | LEAK, water at the tank | Hold E on the tank. | No refills while it leaks, then it runs dry for a minute. The puddle trips sprinters. |
| Drive-by | Tyres outside, DRIVE-BY | Crouch, or get behind a crate or a wall. | Six seconds of shooting: workers hit go down and drop what they hold, growing trays lose growth. |
| Rat | RAT, squeaking | Walk up to it. | It eats a tray's growth for up to 30 s. |
| The phone | Ringing, PHONE | Hold E on it: a tip about the next trouble, cheap seeds for a minute, or a wrong number. | $30 out of cash on hand. |
| Sprinklers | SPRINKLERS, every tray watered | Walk. Do not sprint. | Sprint on the wet floor and you go down and drop it. |
| A plant gets up | A ready tray twitches: "GrowPlot 3 is moving." | Harvest it with empty hands inside six seconds. | It walks out, eats trays and bites. Only fire stops it (below). |
| Black Damp spores | Grey motes over a ripening tray | Crouch when you harvest it. Do not throw things at it. | Grey screen, muffled ears and a cough for 6 s (3 s crouched). |

Three write-ups in a shift and the Boss keeps you in the back room for 15 seconds. You watch the floor (A / D) and
hear it faintly; the floor does not hear you. The flamethrower is in the red cabinet next to the fuse box: E with
empty hands breaks the glass ($40 out of cash on hand, a write-up if nothing is loose), hold left mouse to fire,
eight seconds of fuel, a new one after 90 seconds. Burning a co-worker, or a crop with nothing loose, is a write-up.
Night Shift walks most often, Budget Bud never.

## OPTIONS
On the main menu and on the break card (Esc): mouse speed, invert mouse Y, field of view, fullscreen, window size,
VSync, the first-shift guidance (the Boss names each next step on your first run), sound, master / effects / voice
volume, and the key list. Kept on this PC only. Microphone, push to talk and voice volume are on the break card.

## Voices, radios, gestures
Voices carry about 14 metres and fade. Push to talk (hold V) is on; turn it off on the break card for an open mic.
Two radios sit on a shelf in the corner by the phone. Hold one and talk, and every other radio carries your voice,
wherever it lies. A power cut does not touch them. Gestures: 1 point, 2 a tired wave, 3 shrug, 4 sit down until you
move. Your record (shifts, best shift, the rest) stays on your PC and issues hats; change yours at the locker in the
alley.
